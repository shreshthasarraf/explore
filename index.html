Why Rajasthan?

Few states pack this much into one journey. Heritage is everywhere, and the desert is only the beginning.

Thar DesertGolden dunes around Jaisalmer and Bikaner, camel safaris and nights under the stars.
Heritage everywhereHill forts, palaces, havelis and stepwells in almost every district, six forts on the UNESCO list.
Lakes and hillsUdaipur’s lakes, the ancient Aravalli range and cool green pockets.
WildlifeTigers at Ranthambore and Sariska, birds, leopards and desert fauna.
Art and craftMiniature painting, block printing, blue pottery and folk music.
Food and festivalsDal baati churma, laal maas, kachori, and fairs like Pushkar and Desert Festival.
